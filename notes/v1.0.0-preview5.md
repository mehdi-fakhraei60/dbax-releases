## Bax 1.0.0 preview 5

Native Kotlin + Jetpack Compose. Two apps: **Bax** (customer, `ir.dbax.app`) and **Bax سفیر** (driver, `ir.dbax.app.driver`).

- Every customer screen C01–C38 + camera measurement C07a/b/c, and every driver screen D00–D38
- Live Neshan map (location, map pick + reverse geocoding, search, routing, moving driver marker)
- Kavenegar OTP login for both apps (key server-side only)
- Wallet as a payment method, invoices, guaranteed delivery
- In-app updater on the Account screen (downloads from this public releases repo)
- Driver pay model: entry fee + monthly subscription, fixed pay per 8-hour block, 0% commission
